# Round 4 visual system

The site is composed as a fabrication drawing sheet: deep architectural green, pale material surfaces, and a controlled orange cut-line accent. Bold industrial typography leads; occasional serif phrases soften gallery headings; monospaced labels identify drawings. All fonts are system fonts.

## Five signature motifs

- **Cut line:** an incision through selected headline words, orange leader lines, and fine rules connecting sections.
- **Cut-away frame:** chamfered image corners with contained crops and inset keyboard focus rings.
- **Drawing index:** stable EXT, INT, ORN, SGN, CNC, and MAT identifiers that connect plates to the visual taxonomy.
- **Perforated sheet:** original negative-space geometry, shallow layered shadows, and light surfaces visible through cut masks.
- **Trace and registration:** offset outline studies, sparse grid marks, and crosshair registration details.

Geometry is contemporary and original to this repository. The stepped rhythm introduces a restrained local visual association without naming or claiming an authentic Lampung traditional motif. No copied cultural artwork or invented project history is used.

## Custom components

`CutPathHero` combines the typographic incision and large panel drawing. `CutStudy` produces six reusable SVG studies. `ProjectMosaic` and `ProjectPlate` use unequal scales, offsets, and concise captions. `ApplicationIndex` replaces service boxes with six illustrated rows. `FabricationFlow` links idea, pattern, material, cut, finish, and result as a short diagram. `MaterialStrip` presents five original layered-sheet illustrations. `ProjectCTA` closes with an orange drawing-sheet invitation and verified local contact details.

## Composition and interaction

Portfolio immediately follows the hero. The homepage contains two distinct mosaics, interrupted by the application index and a compact process diagram. The gallery uses a six-part anchor index and native optional detail drawers, not JavaScript filters. Existing service pages retain their deeper crawlable information; the service catalogue is restyled as indexed rows.

Mobile has its own cropped hero artwork, a dominant featured plate followed by staggered pairs, an alternate two-plus-one secondary mosaic, a folded 3-by-2 process, and a 3-plus-2 material sequence. The fixed WhatsApp control is a compact technical tab with safe-area spacing. No animation framework, scroll hijacking, or client scripts are shipped. Reduced-motion preferences disable CSS motion.
